Culture & Heritage

The Storyteller's Route

≈1.5 km · about 2 hrs with stops · includes a steep hill climb

If you like Singapore's history and folklore, follow this route to experience Tales of Earth and Sea and The Lost Legacy of Fort Canning, and learn how the myths of Bugis seafarers and the ancient kingdom of Singapura still shape the city today.

  • 1National Museum of SingaporeMuseum Zone
    Tales of Earth and Sea — facade projection reinterpreting local folklore. Free.
    ↳ Next: take the hill path right behind the museum (~5 min)
  • 2Fort Canning HillMuseum Zone
    The Lost Legacy of Fort Canning: The Forbidden Hill — 70-min trail through Bukit Larangan. $60, select nights. Note: the hill path is a steep climb.
    ↳ Next: walk back downhill and along Armenian St, past the Peranakan Museum (~7 min)
  • 3Armenian StreetMuseum Zone
    Vikings of the Tropics — the maritime myths of Bugis seafarers. Free.
    ↳ Next: cross Stamford Rd at the museum crossing onto the lawn (~6 min)
  • 4SMU Campus GreenMuseum Zone
    The Dragon's Tooth & Traces — light art rooted in Singapore Stone lore. Free.

The Immersive Route

≈2 km · 2–2.5 hrs with stops · flat, stroller-friendly

If you'd rather step inside the story than look at it, follow this route to experience the festival's immersive side — roaming creatures, a horror night in a heritage cinema, and artworks you can touch, add to or wander through. Each stop is tagged with what you'll actually get to do.

The Three Signature Highlights

Short on time? These are the works HeritageSG built this year's festival around — the ones everyone talks about after.

I

Tales of Earth and Sea

A striking facade projection at the National Museum by Indonesian duo The Fox, The Folks, reinterpreting local folklore to an original cross-border soundtrack.

National Museum of Singapore (pin 1) · Free · Launches 21 Aug, 8pm
II

The Lost Legacy of Fort Canning

A 70-minute nocturnal walking trail through the "Forbidden Hill," with soundscapes and live traditional Malay dance.

Fort Canning Hill (pin 2) · $60 · Select nights only
III

MALAM Projection Mapping Competition

The inaugural regional competition — the Top 20 (then Top 5) entries lighting up the historic Cathay facade.

The Cathay (pin 11) · Free
+

Birdmen

Southeast Asian debut of Dutch troupe Close-Act Theatre — huge, luminous pterodactyl-like creatures roaming the streets.

National Museum of Singapore (pin 1) · Free · 21–23 Aug only

Dates & hours

21 Aug – 5 Sep 2026, across three weekends.
Fri & Sat: 7.30pm – 12mn · Sun – Thu: 7.30pm – 11pm

Selected dates only

Not every artwork runs every night — Birdmen roams on the opening weekend only (21–23 Aug), and programmes like The Lost Legacy of Fort Canning, Horror Nights and Legends in Motion run on selected dates. Check the official festival website at heritage.sg/sgnightfest before heading down so your plans match the programme.
